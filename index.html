The problem

There are always 10 more things to do.

Most of it is admin. It isn't the work you're good at, and it isn't what you pay your best people to do.

So the work that grows the business waits until tonight.

MoneyInvoices and getting paid.

Bookkeeping, late payers, expenses, payroll and BAS. Cash-flow problems usually start here.

PaperworkThe paperwork you keep putting off.

Contracts, licences, insurance and safety records. It feels unimportant until you miss one.

CustomersCustomers who never hear back.

The inbox, quotes, bookings and reminders. Good leads go cold because nobody followed up.

MarketingMarketing that never happens.

Social posts, emails and the website. It's never urgent, so it never gets done.

PeopleRosters, leave and new starters.

Slow and draining, and it gets much harder once you pass about 10 staff.

OperationsCopying things from one place to another.

Orders into spreadsheets, reports, hunting for files. Nobody notices it, but it often takes the most time.

    Questions

    Questions you might have.

    How much time will this actually save us?

    It depends on where your hours go, and that's different for every business. That's why we start by sitting down with you to work out where your hours go and what we'd take off your plate first.

    Do we need to be good with technology?

    No. If you can send an email, you can use this. We set everything up, show your team how it works and look after it every month.

    Will it replace my staff?

    It takes the repetitive work off them, so they can spend more time on customers and the work they're best at. What you do with that time is up to you.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    You decide what it does on its own and what it checks with you first. Everything is checked against your rules, and big decisions, like prices and money, always come to you.

    Is our information safe?

    Everything stays in your own accounts, and you decide who can see what. We never sell or share your information.

    Do we have to change how we work?

    No. We build around the tools you already use, like your email, calendar, Xero and your files. Your team keeps working the way it does, with a lot less admin.

    What if we want to stop?

    You own everything we build, and it stays in your accounts. If you stop, you keep it.
